A Voice of Hope Silenced by Violence

Mary Claire, affectionately known as "Clare," is a 24-year-old gospel artist and a proud member of the Redemption Ministers Kenya, a singing group based at the SDA Mabera Main Church in Migori County. She dedicated her life to spreading hope and joy through music.

On Tuesday, March 17, 2026, while walking home from work along Ngong Road in Nairobi with her friend Sarah Watiri, a man identified as Lucas Ochieng' approached her and splashed acid directly onto her face, causing severe burns that have left her fighting for her life.

"Sarah, I'm dying. Get me water. Wash my face." — Those were the words Claire cried out as the acid burned her face and her clothes melted away. — As recounted by her friend Sarah Watiri

Claire is currently receiving treatment at Kenyatta National Hospital. Speaking from her hospital bed, she shared: "Macho moja haioni" (One of my eyes cannot see), revealing the extent of her injuries. Despite the trauma, Claire remains hopeful for a full recovery.

Prominent Supporters

Those Who Stood With Claire

Prominent Kenyan figures who intervened and helped bring the attackers to justice

Mike Sonko

Former Nairobi Governor

Sonko personally coordinated the arrest of Elvis Opiyo, Claire's ex-boyfriend and the alleged mastermind behind the acid attack. He visited Claire in hospital and pledged to cover her medical bills and ensure she receives justice.

Coordinated Arrest & Medical Bills

Sarah Watiri

Claire's Friend & Witness

Sarah was walking with Claire when the attack occurred. Despite sustaining injuries herself, she helped trick Elvis Opiyo into traveling to Nairobi from Homa Bay, leading to his arrest. A true friend who risked her safety for justice.

Helped Trap Suspect

Kibera Police & DCI Officers

Kenya National Police Service

Officers swiftly arrested Lucas Ochieng' at the scene with help from the public. The DCI launched a coordinated investigation, building a case that led to both suspects being detained at Kibera Police Station.

Swift Investigation & Arrest

Kenyan Public & Community

Citizens Who Intervened

Bystanders on Ngong Road cornered Lucas Ochieng' before he could flee, holding him until police arrived. The Kenyan public has rallied behind Claire, with an outpouring of donations, prayers, and calls for justice against acid attacks.
